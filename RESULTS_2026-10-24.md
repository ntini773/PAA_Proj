# Results log: RTX PRO 5000 Blackwell, 2026-10-24

Project: fused LayerNorm + AdaLN + NVFP4 quantization for PixArt-Sigma.
Machine: shared team server, one RTX PRO 5000 (sm_120, 48 GB). Another user's job (17 GB) was on the GPU during the runs.
Stack: torch 2.15.0.dev20260819+cu130, torchao 0.19.0.dev20260920+cu130, triton 3.8.0 (from `pixi.lock`).
Raw data: `results/e2e.jsonl`, `results/images.log`, `results/graph_step.log`, `results/nsys_summary.log`.
Earlier boundary results: `RESULTS_server_partial.md` (same day).

## 1. Summary

1. The fused kernel is **7x to 40x faster than the unfused boundary** in kernel tests.
2. On the **whole transformer step it is 1.49x to 1.81x faster** than stock NVFP4.
3. **Images are identical** between stock and fused at 256, 512 and 1024px.
4. **NVFP4 is slower than bf16 at every size** tested, even with the fused kernel (2.4x slower at 1024px).
5. About **two thirds of the fused step is generic elementwise / reduce / copy kernels** that we did not fuse.
6. **CUDA Graphs add almost nothing** (1.03x to 1.04x). The step is not launch-bound.

## 2. Whole-model result (one denoise step, GPU time, batch 1 with guidance, 14 steps, 2 reps)

| Image | Tokens | bf16 | NVFP4 stock | NVFP4 fused | Fused vs stock | Fused vs bf16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 256px | 256 | 17.8 ms (noisy; seen 8.9 to 17.8) | 99.8 ms | 55.0 ms | **1.81x** faster | about 3x slower |
| 512px | 1024 | 54.8 ms | 157.3 ms | 89.9 ms | **1.75x** faster | 1.6x slower |
| 1024px | 4096 | 236.6 ms | 834.9 ms | 559.2 ms | **1.49x** faster | **2.4x slower** |

- Minimum step times give the same ratios (1.83x, 1.78x, 1.51x).
- Time saved per step: 44.8 ms, 67.4 ms, 275.6 ms. Over 14 steps: 0.63 s, 0.94 s, 3.86 s.
- Wall time per image (stock to fused): 256px 1.43 to 0.80 s, 512px 2.27 to 1.34 s, 1024px 11.95 to 8.09 s.
- Peak memory (fused): 0.68 GB, 1.13 GB, 2.92 GB.
- Fused calls per step: 256. Fallback calls per step: 30 (the 4608-wide MLP second layer and the caption projection).

## 3. Where the time goes (`nsys`, 512px, 2 steps)

| Bucket | Fused | Stock |
|---|---|---|
| Elementwise / reduce / copy | **67.7%** | 78.7% |
| GEMM | 19.6% | 14.4% |
| Attention | 11.4% | 6.8% |
| Our fused quantize kernel | 1.3% | 0% |

- Top "other" kernels are generic PyTorch elementwise, reduce and direct-copy kernels.
- What they are is **not confirmed**. Likely candidates: the per-tensor max, the scale re-layout (`to_blocked`), dtype copies, and the unfused MLP second-layer quantize.
- Caution: total kernel time is 226 ms (fused) vs 159 ms (stock), which contradicts the timed runs. Likely GPU sharing or capture-window effects. Trust the percentages, not the totals.

## 5. What the numbers do not mean

- The 7x, 14x and 10x to 42x figures are **boundary-only**. They are not whole-model speedups.
- The shared GPU may inflate absolute times. Ratios were consistent across sizes, so they are more reliable.
- The bf16 256px number is noisy (8.9 to 17.8 ms). Do not quote it as exact.
- Text embeddings were random numbers. Image content is meaningless. Only stock vs fused is comparable.
- The model run fuses only the quantize step, only for 1152-wide inputs. LayerNorm and modulate stay in PyTorch.
