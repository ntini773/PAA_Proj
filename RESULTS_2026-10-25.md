# Results log: 2026-10-25 (RTX PRO 5000, clean GPU unless noted)

Step time = GPU time of one denoise step (batch 1, with guidance). Text embeddings are random numbers.
"fused" = fused quantize kernel on the 1152-wide layers. "wide" = also the 4608-wide MLP second layer and 4096-wide caption layer (`EXTRA_OK_K=4608,4096`).

## Step time (ms, median)

| Image | bf16 | fp8 | NVFP4 stock | NVFP4 fused | NVFP4 fused + wide | wide vs stock | wide vs bf16 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 256px | 8.3 | 34.1 | 94.0 | 50.9 | 43.7 | 2.15x faster | 5.3x slower |
| 512px | 23.3 | 34.4 | 94.9 | 53.7 | 44.3 | 2.14x faster | 1.9x slower |
| 1024px | 103.1 (min 95.7) | 128.0 | 370.0 | 250.8 | **98.7** (min 97.1) | **3.75x faster** | **tie** (1.04x faster by median, 1.5% slower by min) |

- Stock vs wide-fused images: mean and max difference **0.0** at all three sizes.
- fp16 produced NaN images (numerical overflow); its step time is not meaningful for quality.
- At 1024px the fused-only run spent 156.8 of 163.7 ms of "quantize kernel" time in the unfused MLP second layer. Fusing it removed about 150 ms per step.

| 1024px | fused | 255.3 | 206.4 | 254.9 | 1.00x |

At 256px and 512px the CPU launch time equals the step time: NVFP4 there is limited by the CPU launching kernels (Python and torchao overhead), not by the GPU. At 1024px the GPU is the limit.

## Layer and stage costs (see README for the tables)
- Stock quantize is 83% to 87% of the unfused LayerNorm + modulate + quantize chain's GPU time; it moves about 41x the ideal DRAM traffic and runs 41 kernels.
- Fused quantize stage is 12x to 28x faster, byte-identical.
- Widths 768 to 3072 (SD3.5, Flux sizes): stock quantize 0.21 to 0.35 ms, fused 0.036 to 0.040 ms.

## Open issues
- Numerical drift vs bf16 on random embeddings: fp8 MAE about 0.10, NVFP4 MAE 0.14 to 0.21 (PSNR 10 to 13 dB). Needs a real-prompt check before claiming NVFP4 quality is acceptable.
- `torch.compile` baseline failed (`_repeated_blocks` empty). Fixed in `scale_e2e.py` (untested).
- MSLK (torchao's own fast quantize kernel) could not be installed: the pixi env has no pip. Not tested.
- Unlabeled lines in the old results suggest batch 4 and 8 at 256px (stock 96 / 168 ms, fused 51 / 102 ms) and batch 4 at 512px (357 / 231 ms). Batch 4 at 256px costs the same as batch 1, which fits a CPU-bound step.
